About to install Kairos
=======================

Kairos transcribes recorded meetings, labels who spoke, extracts the action
items, decisions, deadlines, issues and risks, and exports a Word report.

Everything runs on this computer. Recordings are never uploaded anywhere.


WHAT THIS SETUP WILL DO
-----------------------

  1. Copy Kairos into your own user folder.
     No administrator password is needed, and nothing outside your account
     is changed.

  2. Install anything that is missing.
     Kairos needs Python, Node.js, FFmpeg and Ollama. Setup checks for each
     one and installs only what you do not already have.

  3. Download the AI model (about 4.7 GB).
     This is optional - you can untick it on the next screen and download it
     later instead.

  4. Ask for a Hugging Face token.
     This is a free account key that lets Kairos download the models that
     tell speakers apart. You can leave it blank now and add it later.


BEFORE YOU CONTINUE, PLEASE CHECK
---------------------------------

  * You have an internet connection. The downloads are several gigabytes.

  * You have about 40 GB of free disk space.

  * You have 20 to 40 minutes. Most of that is downloading, and it only
    happens on this first install.

A graphics card is not required. If this computer does not have an NVIDIA
graphics card, Kairos switches to a slower mode automatically and still
works normally.


AFTER INSTALLING
----------------

Kairos appears in your Start Menu. Open it, wait for the two black windows
to finish starting, then go to http://localhost:5173 in your browser.

To remove Kairos later, use Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps. Please
note that uninstalling also deletes any meetings you have processed, so
export anything you want to keep first.


Click Next to continue, or Cancel to stop without changing anything.
