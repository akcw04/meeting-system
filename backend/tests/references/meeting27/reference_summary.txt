The Medford City Council meeting opened with a roll call, the salute to the flag, and approval of prior meeting records and committee reports, including a minor spelling correction to the records. The substance of the meeting was the Fiscal Year 2027 budget. Councillors debated pay equity, raising concerns that librarians and paraprofessionals face a gender pay gap and that the proposed wage increases were inadequate. Transport was discussed in relation to the MBTA bus network redesign affecting Medford, including earlier advocacy that preserved bus route 80 and improved service frequency. The mayor's office signalled a willingness to add over one hundred and twelve thousand dollars to the proposed budget for recreation, arts, library, legislative and law services, conditional on a package budget deal, and reported a fifty thousand dollar Tufts grant to the Medford Family Network and a one hundred thousand dollar Cummings grant. The meeting became contentious over funding for KP Law, with councillors arguing that such budget decisions should be debated in public rather than behind closed doors.
