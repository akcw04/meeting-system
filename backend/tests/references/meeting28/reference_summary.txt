The team met to confirm the product launch plan. The payment vendor had not yet given final confirmation, raising the risk that users could face payment issues if the vendor delays further. Weighing this risk, the team decided to postpone the launch by one week. On training, the support staff session had only 6 of 12 members attend, so a follow-up session was scheduled for Thursday, and it was agreed that all support staff must complete training before launch. Engineering had load-tested only up to 5,000 concurrent users against an expected 8,000, so another load test was requested with updated results due by Friday. The team also agreed to follow up with the payment vendor today.
