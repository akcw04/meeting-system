A regional airport board meeting opened with apologies and the standard procedural business: appointing movers and seconders for motions, disclosure of members' interests, confirmation of the order of business, and confirmation of the previous meeting's minutes, during which a correction was noted because a member's attendance had not been recorded. The main business concerned the organisation's financial position and its recovery from the COVID-19 pandemic. A presentation addressed local tourism operators that depend on international visitors, and members discussed the loss of aeronautical income, the critical role of non-aeronautical revenue in offsetting that loss, and a recovery expected to be modest but sustainable. The board also reviewed its borrowing position, noting a favourable interest rate of around 1.7 percent, and agreed to continue monitoring conditions before closing.
